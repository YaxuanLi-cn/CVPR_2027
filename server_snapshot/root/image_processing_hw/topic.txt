[2] Which image quality metric should we use for
novel view synthesis?
NeRF and 3D Gaussian Splatting papers almost always report PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS on held-out
views. However, these methods produce artefacts that differ from classical distortions, such as
floaters, blurry backgrounds, needle-like splats and view-dependent flicker, and it is unclear which
metrics capture them. Several subjective datasets of neural view-synthesis quality have recently been
published (e.g., Liang et al. 2023, on front-facing NeRF views).
You will train (or download pre-trained) 3DGS and/or NeRF models on a few scenes. You will
generate degraded variants, for example by training with fewer input views, fewer iterations or fewer
Gaussians, and render the test views or camera paths. The research part is to compare how well
common metrics (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FLIP, ColorVideoVDP) agree with each other and with
subjective data. That data can come from a public dataset (e.g., this one). You should also check
whether video metrics capture temporal artefacts that per-frame metrics miss.
Associated lectures: Image-based rendering (NeRF, Gaussian splatting); Assessing image quality
(metrics, pairwise comparisons, scaling)
